The Malahang Difference

Academic Knowledge Meets Practical Skills

Malahang Technical Secondary School has developed a distinctive model that brings academic secondary education together with technical and trade-oriented learning. Public reporting describes students progressing through Grades 9–12 while developing an additional technical skill pathway.

Reported training areas have included tourism and hospitality, catering, garment technology, carpentry, cabinet and furniture making, welding, metal fabrication, electrical, auto electrical, motor mechanics, panel beating and spray painting. Fisheries Skills Technology and Heavy Equipment Fitter training have also been introduced as additional programmes.
